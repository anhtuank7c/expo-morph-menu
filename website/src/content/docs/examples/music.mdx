### How it works

1. The `Trigger` slot is four bars that keep moving while the button is closed and shrink to nothing as `progress` rises.
2. The slot only animates its own content; the package animates the button growing into the card.
3. `surface: 'solid'` with `colors.dark.surface` gives the card a deep tone on every platform.
