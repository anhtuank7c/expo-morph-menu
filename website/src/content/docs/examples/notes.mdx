### How it works

1. `anchor: 'bottom-left'` moves the button to the left corner; the card opens from there.
2. The `Item` slot shows a shortcut chip, and the `Separator` slot draws hairlines between rows (`layout.separatorHeight`).
3. `colors.light` sets the paper tones of the card, the text and the screens.
4. The voice memo animates a waveform with Reanimated; the checklist ticks items off.
