### How it works

1. An `Item` slot lays out an icon tile, the service, its ETA and a price, all from `item.data`; `itemHeight` makes every row 64 high.
2. A `Background` slot draws a white card with a green band at the top, the same in the card and while it grows into the screen.
3. The Ride screen draws a map with plain views and lets you pick a car; Groceries counts what you add.
