### How it works

1. Tap + to open the card, then a row: the card grows into the route `/(sheet)/[key]`. Or press and hold +, slide onto a row and release.
2. `MorphProvider` in the root layout holds the items; `<MorphMenu />` after the `Stack` draws the button and the card over every screen.
3. The `(sheet)` group is registered with `morphSheetScreenOptions`, and its layout is `MorphSheetLayout`: every route inside expands from the card.
4. The screen renders a skeleton until `useMorphSettled()` is true, so the expansion never waits for heavy content. `onHover` prefetches the data of the row under the pointer.
5. Close with the × (`MorphCloseButton`), the browser back or Esc: the screen collapses into the card, and the menu stays open.
